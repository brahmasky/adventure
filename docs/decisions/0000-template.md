# ADR NNNN: <short decision title>

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR-XXXX](xxxx-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** <who>

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, constraints, and what made a decision necessary.
State the facts neutrally — someone reading this in a year should understand *why a
choice had to be made* without the surrounding conversation.

## Decision

What we decided, stated plainly and in the active voice ("We will …"). Include the
key parameters/values that make the decision concrete.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or constrained as a result — the trade-offs we
accepted, the risks we took on, and what this commits future work to. Note any
follow-up or revisit conditions.

## Alternatives considered

Briefly, the options we rejected and why — so the decision isn't re-litigated from
scratch later.
